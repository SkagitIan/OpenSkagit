    Frequently asked questions
    What does this alert do?
    We watch county recorder updates for the parcel numbers you pick and email you when new important filings show up.
    What kinds of filings can send an email?
    Mostly deeds, quitclaim deeds, liens, lien releases, and powers of attorney.
    How often do you check for changes?
    Once each night. If something important is new, we send an email.
    Will I get emails about old records from before I signed up?
    No. We start from when you sign up and alert you on newer filings.
    How many parcels can I track?
    You can track up to {{ max_active_watches }} parcels per email address.
    How do I edit, pause, or delete my alerts?
    Use the Manage, Unsubscribe, or Delete links in any alert email we send you.
    Is this the county government or legal advice?
    No. OpenSkagit is independent. This tool shares public-record updates and does not provide legal advice.
