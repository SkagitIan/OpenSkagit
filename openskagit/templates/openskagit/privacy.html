Privacy

Privacy Policy

Plain-language notes on how OpenSkagit handles the information you share and the public records that power this site.

Last updated: December 2025

What we collect

  • Contact details you submit

    Emails, messages, or business descriptions sent through contact or consult forms.

  • Uploads and inputs

    Files or text you provide when asking for analysis (for example, parcel documents) so we can process the request.

  • Operational logs

    Standard web logs (IP address, browser, timestamps, error traces) that help keep the site reliable and secure.

How we use it

  • To respond

    We use your contact details to reply to questions, schedule follow-ups, or deliver the analysis you requested.

  • To improve the tools

    Aggregated, non-identifying usage trends help prioritize fixes and features.

  • To send opted-in updates

    Briefings or newsletters are sent only when you opt in, and every email includes an unsubscribe link.

AI features and data processors

When you use AI-backed tools (like parcel insights), the text and structured inputs you provide may be processed by trusted vendors such as OpenAI to generate a response. We only send the minimum context needed for the request and do not allow providers to reuse inputs for training.

Public-record data (parcels, zoning, tax records, elections, menus) comes from government and commercial datasets but OpenSkagit is not affiliated with Skagit County Government.

Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies for session management and form protection. Analytics are limited to understanding site reliability and aggregate usage; we do not run ad trackers or sell browsing data.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time using the link in briefing or newsletter emails.

  • Request deletion of contact submissions or uploaded files by emailing hello@openskagit.com.

  • Ask questions about how your data is handled—we prefer direct, clear answers over fine print.

Questions?

Email hello@openskagit.com or use the contact form. We will respond with the specific steps taken for your request.
