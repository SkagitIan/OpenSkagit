What

Tax vs inflation

—

Tax CAGR vs CPI baseline.

What

Shows how fast your taxes have grown over time compared to inflation (CPI). If your tax growth rate beats CPI, your tax burden is rising faster than the cost of living. This is the cleanest "is this getting harder to afford?" signal.

How it's calculated

Tax CAGR over the selected period vs CPI CAGR over the same period.

Effective tax rate (ETR)

—

Taxes as a share of market value.

Vs county median: —

What

Your tax bill as a percent of market value - think "tax yield" on your property. ETR lets you compare across very different home prices and quickly see whether your parcel is taxed heavier or lighter than typical.

How it's calculated

ETR = annual taxes / market value (shown as a %).

Example

Example: —

County median ETR

—

Skagit median effective tax rate.

What

The countywide midpoint ETR for the current tax year (half of parcels are above, half below). This is the benchmark for "normal" and makes your ETR meaningful in context. A small difference matters when it persists year after year.

How it's calculated

Median of annual taxes / market value across all parcels for the year.

Vertical equity

—

ETR by home value bracket.

Value brackets County median: —

What

Checks whether tax burden is fair across value levels (lower-value vs higher-value properties). "Regressive" means lower-value homes pay a higher effective rate than higher-value homes; "progressive" is the opposite. This is a fairness lens on the system, not just your parcel.

How it's calculated

Compare ETR by value bracket; the pattern across brackets determines progressive vs proportional vs regressive.
