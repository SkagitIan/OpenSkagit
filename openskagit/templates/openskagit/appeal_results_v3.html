What is COD?

COD measures horizontal equity—how consistent assessments are among similar properties. Lower values mean tighter groupings.

What is PRD?

PRD highlights whether high-value parcels are assessed differently than low-value parcels. Values above 1.03 suggest regressivity, below 0.98 suggest progressivity.
